01 VOICE RANGE: SCRIPT

The same three lines for every take. Only the mood changes.

ENGLISH
  1. Some moments only happen once.
  2. Make sure they're heard exactly the way they should be.
  3. This is the voice for it.

FILIPINO
  1. May mga sandaling minsan lang mangyari.
  2. Siguraduhin nating maririnig ito sa tamang paraan.
  3. Ito ang boses para diyan.

(Change a word if it does not sound like you. Keep the meaning.)

THE TAKES
  01 WARM          Like talking to a close friend. Smile in the voice.
  02 ENERGETIC     Big and bright. A host who gets the room moving.
  03 SERIOUS       Steady, no smile. Corporate, trustworthy.
  04 DRAMATIC      Weight on the key words. Use pauses.
  05 CALM          Soft and relaxed. Unhurried, comforting.
  06 FRIENDLY      Casual, everyday, conversational.
  07 AUTHORITATIVE Confident and commanding. An announcement people obey.
  08 CINEMATIC     Deep and slow, like a movie trailer. Let it build.
  09 EMOTIONAL     Heartfelt, close to the mic. It is fine if the last line softens.
  10 FAST PACE     Quick and crisp, like a short ad. Still clear.
  11 SLOW PACE     Deliberate. Every word given room.

Say the take name before each take, for example: "Cinematic, English, take one."
