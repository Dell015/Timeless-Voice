01 VOICE RANGE

Purpose: show how flexible your voice is. The same lines, read in different moods and paces.
This becomes the "range" part of the reel, so this is the most important recording.

What to do
- Read the three lines in _SCRIPT.txt once for each take on the list (11 takes).
- Do each take in English, then again in Filipino (or Taglish, whichever feels natural). 22 takes in total.
- Do 2 takes of each. It takes about 20 to 30 minutes.

File names: 01_warm_EN_take1.m4a, 01_warm_FIL_take1.m4a, 02_energetic_EN_take1.m4a ... and so on.
Use the take number from the list.

Phone recording is fine. See READ-ME-FIRST for tips.
