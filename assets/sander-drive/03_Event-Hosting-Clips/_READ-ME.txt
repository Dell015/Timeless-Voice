03 EVENT HOSTING CLIPS

Purpose: show you hosting. Real events are best.

Upload
- Video clips of you hosting: weddings, debuts, corporate events, programs, school events, pageants.
- 15 to 60 seconds each is perfect. Longer is OK. Pick moments where you are talking and the crowd reacts.
- Include a strong opening, a moment where the crowd reacts, and a closing.
- Original video files, not downloaded from Facebook, if you can.

Ask the client or organizer before sending. We will confirm again before we publish.

If you have no footage yet, record the optional lines in _SCRIPT.txt on video, standing, as if the room is full.
