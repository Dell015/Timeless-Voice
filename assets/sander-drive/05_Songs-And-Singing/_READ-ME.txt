05 SONGS AND SINGING

Purpose: show that your voice can sing and hold a tune, so people can hire you for songs, jingles and vocals.

Upload
- 30 to 60 second clips of songs, jingles, covers, backing vocals, demos. Without music is fine (a cappella).
- Original songs and jingles are best.
- Covers: you can upload them for us to hear. A cover of a copyrighted song cannot be used in a boosted ad
  unless the song is licensed, so we may not be able to use it in the reel.
- Optional: sing the three lines from folder 01 in any style you like.
