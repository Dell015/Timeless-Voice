SIR SANDER: PLEASE READ THIS FIRST

What this is
We are making a reel to show people what your voice can do, and a website to book you.
We need your recordings, videos and photos to build them. This Drive is where you put them.

How it works
1. Open each numbered folder. Each has a file called _READ-ME.txt. It says what to upload.
2. Some folders also have _SCRIPT.txt. Read the lines as written, but if a line feels unnatural,
   say it the way you would naturally say it. English, Filipino or mixed is all fine.
3. Upload your files into the right folder. If you are not sure, put it in 11_Anything-Else.
4. If you have no past work for a folder, you can record the demo lines in that folder's script.
   If you do have past work, upload that first. Real work is better than a demo.

Please only upload what you are allowed to share
- Work you did for a client: OK to upload for us to look at. We will ask the client before we use it.
- Do not upload music you do not own or have a licence for. We cannot use it.
- Do not upload private information about clients (addresses, IDs, payments).

FILE NAMES
Use this pattern: type_client-or-event_year
Examples: ad_bahay-kape_2025.wav   host_wedding-santos_2024.mp4   warm_EN_take1.m4a
If you do not remember the year, leave it out.

RECORDING TIPS (a phone is fine)
- Quiet room. A small room with soft things (clothes, blankets, curtains) sounds better than a big empty one. Turn off fans, aircon and phone alerts.
- Phone about a hand's width (15 to 20 cm) from your mouth, a little off to the side so you don't pop on P and B sounds.
- Use the phone's voice recorder at the highest quality. No music, no effects, no filters.
- Keep the same distance and volume for every take.
- Leave 2 seconds of silence at the start and end of each take.
- Say the take name out loud before each take (for example: "Warm, take one").
- Do 2 takes of each. We pick the best.
- Upload the original files from the phone. Do NOT send them through Messenger or chat first, because that lowers the quality.

Thank you, Sir. If anything is unclear, ask us. Do not skip a folder because of a question.
