08 TESTIMONIALS AND PERMISSIONS

Purpose: reviews from clients, and proof that clients allow us to reuse their video or voice.
We will not publish a client's work without permission.

Upload
- Written reviews (screenshots of messages are fine, with the name visible if the client agrees).
- Short video reviews if a client is willing.
- Screenshots of messages where a client says "yes, you can use it".

Use MESSAGE-TEMPLATES.txt to ask clients. Send it to clients from past events.
