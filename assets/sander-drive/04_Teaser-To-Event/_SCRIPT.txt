04 TEASER TO EVENT: SCRIPT
Record in the same voice and mood for both parts. 2 takes each.

PART 1: THE TEASER (cinematic, about 20 seconds)
  "One night. One celebration. Everyone you love, in one room.
   The Evening of Gold. This Saturday."

PART 2: THE EVENT OPENING (warm and confident, about 20 seconds)
  "Good evening, everyone. Yes, you've heard this voice before. Welcome to the night we've all been waiting for."
