02 VOICE-OVER SAMPLES

Purpose: show the many things your voice can be hired for, including things people do not
think of. There is a subfolder for each kind of job.

What to upload
- Option A (best): past work. Ads, explainers, corporate videos, narration, phone greetings,
  audiobook reads, and also school, personal and creative projects you have voiced.
- Option B: if you have nothing for a subfolder, record the demo lines in _SCRIPT.txt.

Subfolders
  Ads
  Explainers-And-Corporate
  Narration-And-Documentary
  Phone-And-IVR
  Audiobooks-And-Stories
  Schools-And-Education
  Personal-Projects
  Creative-Projects
  Other-Uses

Ideas people can hire a voice for, especially schools and creative people
  Schools: graduation messages, foundation day and intramurals openings, morning announcements,
    quiz bee announcer, school play narration, campus tour videos, lesson and e-learning narration,
    school AVPs, thesis and project video narration for students.
  Personal: birthday and anniversary greetings, wedding AVPs, proposal messages, tributes,
    gift messages, memory videos.
  Creative: podcast intros, YouTube and stream intros, vlogs, short film narration, poems and stories,
    game characters, trailers, school and student film projects, small business product videos.
  If you have done something else, put it in Other-Uses with a one-line note.

File names: type_client-or-event_year. Example: school_graduation-message_2025.wav
